## Incident and breach response

1. **Receive and record.** Establish monitored reporting/alerts first. Record awareness, investigation and confirmation times, affected environments, responder and evidence references. Distinguish suspicion from confirmed breach without delaying investigation. Use a restricted incident record outside the affected database; the current CRM has no incident case/notification module.
2. **Contain safely.** Security owner limits affected access and preserves evidence. Implemented account suspension and role changes revoke sessions. Revoke exposed provider credentials through their owners, restrict affected connectors and isolate infrastructure where necessary. Do not delete audit/evidence during containment; changing a bootstrap variable does not reset an existing account password.
3. **Investigate and preserve.** Preserve relevant application/provider logs, time boundaries, access paths and affected record categories with controlled custody. Identify confidentiality, integrity and availability impact and whether education/employee/payment data is involved. Use approved encrypted evidence storage and minimize copies/location exposure.
4. **Notify the LEA.** Under supplied DPA 5.4, Provider provides notice within **72 hours of confirmation**, subject to its stated lawful investigation-delay exception. Privacy owner evaluates other applicable obligations and any shorter controlling agreement/law. Notify through agreed written channel, with provider contact, notice date, breach date/time range if known, general description, affected data categories and affected individuals as required, plus lawful-delay explanation when applicable. Include known facts, uncertainty, containment and next update; update without overstating certainty. This document sends no notice.
5. **Coordinate.** LEA handles required parent/guardian/student notifications under the supplied DPA; Provider supports its investigation and notices. LEA's separate 3.4 reciprocal duty is not a substitute for Provider notice. Route regulatory/legal/media questions through authorized privacy/legal owners.
6. **Recover and reconcile.** Fix the cause, verify contained access and inspect business/audit integrity. Restore only through a tested process; reconcile gift/pledge/grant/time totals, users/scopes and activity. Verify old sessions cannot resume and connectors cannot duplicate actions before reopening.
7. **Close and prevent recurrence.** Document root cause, affected scope, notices, corrective controls, evidence and responsible owner. Perform the post-breach security assessment required by 5.2 and obtain district closure as appropriate. For Utah Exhibit G privacy-violation notice, track the **30-day remedy, preventive-process and documentation duties** and prescribed termination process if unremedied; contract terms must not impose a penalty for that privacy termination.

## Backup and disaster recovery
