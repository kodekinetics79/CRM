# Everbright phase two visual extension

User explicitly requested more design depth, color combinations, typography, gradients, 3D, glass and animation. This authorizes extending the established School fund ledger world. Keep navy navigation, white record surfaces, readable data and familiar controls; enrich those primitives rather than replace the product topology.

Self-host IBM Plex Sans for staff controls and data, paired with Newsreader for editorial headings. Fonts are supplied from their Fontsource packages with bundled OFL licenses. Primary references: https://github.com/IBM/plex and https://github.com/productiontype/NewsReader.

The giving chart is the focal dimensional surface: SVG front/top/side geometry, bounded gradient illumination, a dark data stage, and a glass inspector carrying exact monthly amounts. Range and depth controls and monthly selections are real; Explore gifts drills into the corresponding recorded period. Perspective never determines financial values; height represents the actual saved monetary amount. Fees/noncash/voids are explicitly excluded. One brief clip reveal introduces the dimensional bars; selection colors and shadows explain inspection. Reduced motion removes the reveal. No animation loops or sound.

Glass in the sticky working toolbar lets scrolled records establish spatial continuity, with a legible opaque fallback. Ambient surface shadows separate white working panels from the cool canvas. Warm gold belongs to the Everbright sunrise mark; blue identifies actions and selected chart data; teal identifies other monetary periods.
