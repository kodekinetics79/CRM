# Phase-four operations and phase-five local integrity acceptance

Release0.4.0, September 13, 2026. Scope: local synthetic evaluator. This is owner-run functional verification, not independent institutional certification.

## Delivered

Operations opens a cross-module Work queue, Volunteer shifts and Data quality review. Work items link to actual source records; completed tasks are excluded; pledge due values derive from qualifying posted receipts; grant reporting dates ask for review without inferring completion; eligible acknowledgments exclude future/void/fee/opted-out records.

Staff and administrators plan same-day shifts with a venue, shared foundation-local scheduling times, capacity, optional event and Open/Closed state. Reserved/Waitlisted/Cancelled history links to eligible people. Mutation transactions enforce capacity, one active place per person/shift and nonoverlapping reserved intervals. Cancelled history remains; explicit waitlist promotion checks available capacity and conflicts again. Reservation identity cannot change, referenced shifts/people cannot be deleted, and dates/times/event are locked after reservation history exists. Bookings do not create attendance, hours, guest registration or messages.

Shared-email review normalizes exact nonempty addresses and opens constituent history. It never merges records or claims to detect every duplicate. Administrator Workspace checks query current local record fields, links and constraints plus simultaneous volunteer clocks, showing concrete issues without changing records/audits or testing external providers.

Frosted navigation/search/context adds depth with opaque fallbacks and reduced-transparency support. Data planes retain solid backgrounds, right-aligned tabular numbers, one accent, keyboard focus and fast transitions. Eleven Client testing scenarios now include reservation recovery and source-record work journeys.
