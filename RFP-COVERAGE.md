This document maps Jordan Everbright, Kode Kinetics' local synthetic evaluator pilot tailored to Jordan Education Foundation, to Jordan School District RFP 27TH05P5. The name does not imply district endorsement. RFP instructions are procurement requirements to analyze; this document is not a bid submission or a statement that those requirements have been satisfied.

“Demonstrated” below means a local workflow using synthetic data and the pilot's application/database. It does not mean production certification, buyer acceptance, a live external integration, or full parity with every scored criterion.

## Procurement gates this code cannot satisfy

The phase-two workflows extend actual local functionality; see [PHASE-TWO-ACCEPTANCE.md](./PHASE-TWO-ACCEPTANCE.md) for acceptance evidence and limitations. They do not establish full coverage of each scored criterion or production readiness.

The original mandatory criteria require at least three years of relevant product/services experience and three qualifying references (RFP p.12). Official Q&A #32 permits US higher-education experience and references. Q&A #60 permits the software provider's qualifying experience/references; implementation personnel experience alone does not establish platform qualification. Building this pilot creates neither the required history nor references.

Addendum 2 p.1 restricts headquarters to the United States or Canada and collected data storage to those countries. It also references US-based personnel/office requirements and a Canadian corporation exception. A local synthetic deployment does not establish the bidder's personnel location or production hosting commitments.

The mandatory criteria also include iBoss compatibility, the signed boycott statement, the student data privacy agreement, Learn Platform approval, and the prescribed direct-provider/reseller arrangements (RFP pp.12–13). None is certified by this pilot. Partner or reseller submissions require the buyer-prescribed software-provider letters; see official Q&A #62 and #85.

Official Q&A #33 does not prohibit building from scratch, but relevant references, a working presentation-stage demo, and implementation timing still matter. The November 1, 2026 desired start (RFP p.14) requires a separate, credible delivery and migration plan.

## Submission and scoring context

The supplied RFP/addenda list a proposal deadline of September 18, 2026 at noon MDT, with electronic submission through U3P Bonfire (RFP pp.1, 10; addenda p.1). The portal's latest official notices remain the source of truth. This project has not been submitted to the buyer.

The RFP requires separate cost and technical submissions, excludes pricing from the technical proposal, prescribes response sections and a 100-page limit with exclusions, and requires a 75% technical threshold: 2,625 of 3,500 points (RFP pp.10, 12, 21–22, 29–30). This matrix is a review aid, not the complete criterion-by-criterion technical response and not a prediction of awarded points.

## Release0.4.0 additions

Operations links recorded tasks, grant dates, pledge due balances and eligible acknowledgments to supporting records. Staff-managed volunteer shifts deliver explicit capacity, waitlists, cancellation history, conflict protection and constituent/event links. Shared-email review and administrator-run local workspace integrity checks are delivered. These extend A1/A5/A8 and implementation evaluation without establishing public self-service, live integration or institutional approval. See PHASE-FOUR-ACCEPTANCE.md and CTO-PROJECT-PLAN.md.

## Fresh full-document alignment review

September 13, 2026: the exact 31-page base RFP was reopened and compared with release0.4.0 source and evidence. The 84-criterion matrix in RFP-Alignment-Review-0.4.0.md distinguishes local, partial, missing and delivery-evidence coverage. It prioritizes required functionality and conversion ahead of optional visual/advanced additions. In particular, minimum 4–6-user acceptance, many-designation scale, multiple-location subaccounts, native Excel/report PDF, constituent role/household management and sponsorship/event attribution remain explicit gaps rather than being inferred from fields or generic reports.

RFP p.10 excludes ZIP/embedded-file proposal uploads. The client source ZIP is a local evaluation handover only, not a compliant proposal attachment. Localhost is not externally reachable; external evaluation delivery must be established separately. This fresh review did not modify runtime or repeat unchanged tests and did not certify the final live portal notice set.
