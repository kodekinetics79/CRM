## Recommended competitive posture

**Win through admissibility, easier communications and report creation, and a credible conversion/service commitment.** The current Wimblo evaluator has meaningful saved workflows and controls, but a working POC cannot cure missing mandatory experience/references or demonstrate an approved institutional service. The immediate commercial decision is whether a truthful eligible offer structure and a deliverable November 1 scope can be evidenced before submission. Q38 identifies complicated communications and custom report creation as pain points while describing existing reports as good and robust. Comfortable users, easy management/manual imports/exports, accurate accessible data and seamless communications define success. Wimblo must prove improvement in those operating tasks; additional visual polish, AI and subscription controls receive no priority over those gates.

## Presentation plan if invited

Use the buyer's presentation requirements and allotted time. Build an evidence-linked walkthrough; the following sequence is a proposed allocation of emphasis, not a substitute for the official format.

1. State offered product, provider/implementer, included scope and deployment/approval boundaries. Confirm the qualification and delivery evidence before leading with optional features.
2. Record/import a gift with several school/classroom allocations. Show invalid split rejection, saved donor history, pledge fulfillment and financial report control totals. Explain the disconnected-processor route.
3. Record a community-grant request, award and receipt; attempt wrong-funder/over-award linkage; show receipt void reversal without deleting history.
4. Demonstrate the exact required custom report builder and formats when implemented. If a report is scheduled later, show the proven builder and committed timeline required by Q86. Do not present static fixed reports as that builder.
5. Show constituent/household/subsidiary attribution and contact preference handling. Demonstrate report creation comfort, July-to-June fiscal boundaries, authorized year reassignment and scheduled internal or email output when implemented. Complete a manual acknowledgment and open its linked log; identify actual delivery as a separate provider workflow.
6. Demonstrate the required full/data-entry users and optional board access, export/read restrictions and immediate suspension. Show only capabilities available in the evaluated configuration.
7. Present actual trial conversion reconciliation, launch milestones, recovery rehearsal and assigned training/support commitments. End with included commercial scope in the format permitted by the buyer.

Under Q17, AI is optional and requires human review/disclosure. AI may follow these required workflows only if permitted data/provider configuration and review are demonstrated. Blackbaud, Bloomerang and HubSpot already publish AI capabilities; claiming uniqueness is indefensible. Wimblo's AI drafts/suggestions and platform plan controls do not establish automated fundraising results, certified privacy or payment processing. Keep saved draft/action and external delivery distinct.
