# Jordan Everbright — product ownership and expansion plan

September 13, 2026. This plan governs delivery beyond the phase-three synthetic evaluator. Kode Kinetics owns commercial commitments; the assistant acts as implementation and delivery coordinator within this authorized local workspace. It does not speak for the district, sign agreements or authorize external integrations.

## Product decisions

Preserve the foundation team's mental model: record a fact once, connect supporting history, expose the next useful action, and keep plans distinct from completed activity. A gift is recorded income, not a payment attempt; an award or pledge is a commitment; a reservation is a place, not attendance or hours; a logged acknowledgment is a staff action, not verified message delivery. Never manufacture an institutional approval or qualifying product history through a demonstration.

Operations provides a starting point across existing modules. Work items open supporting records. Deadlines and overdue pledge amounts are derived, not guessed. Grant reporting dates require review because completed reports are not yet recorded. Shared email identifies a review group, not proof that two people are duplicates.

The current release is 0.4.0. Glass is applied to navigation and contextual layers with opaque fallbacks. Tables, money and editors retain solid readable surfaces, indigo action/selection, visible focus and short motion. Expand features through separate record collections and validated links, rather than mixing reservations, financial entries and time history into one model.

## Release discipline

Every release must update coverage and limitations, build successfully, run the existing regression suite plus meaningful tests for new rules, walk changed journeys including a recovery and permission path, inspect desktop/mobile together, and produce a clean source/prebuilt archive without databases, session/environment files or installed dependencies. Re-run journeys after functional changes, newly discovered defects or integration configuration; repeating an unchanged pass without a new question is not evidence of additional readiness.

A user-journey pass records setup, action, actual saved result and recovery. Include reload/restart when persistence matters. Seek independent client observations through Client testing; developer/browser checks alone do not establish the real client's first-glance comprehension.

The chief, controls and security reviews from phase three remain governing business boundaries. Fresh additional agent dispatch for phase four reached the session's agent limit; phase-four implementation/testing evidence is owner-run, not falsely labeled independent SME certification. Obtain a fresh independent review before making a production commitment.

## Evidence needed to advance external phases

Approve the source data inventory and fiscal/scheduling policy; provide sandbox provider accounts through an approved secret mechanism; define allowable data and integration scopes; agree deployment regions/identity/support/recovery targets and delivery commitments; obtain required procurement/provider/reference and district privacy/security approvals. No production or payment commitment is made in the absence of this evidence.
