The following seven cards are **market-alternative battlecards**, not a purported bidder roster. ## Common comparison rules

Use the same synthetic donor, school/classroom allocations, household/subsidiary relationships, pledge, community grant, acknowledgment and export reconciliation cases across alternatives when a comparison is authorized. Ask the evaluated implementation to demonstrate saved outcomes, actual role restrictions, conversion and reporting. A capability absent from a marketing page is an unanswered proof question, not evidence that the product lacks it.

Q86's district-processor restriction changes the comparison: the district has no Stripe account and its processor cannot connect to the CRM. Require a workable approved record/import/reconciliation approach without promising a forbidden connection. Quote four full users plus two data-entry users, and optional board access at no additional cost. Keep community-grant scope, November 1 launch and November 30 incumbent download cutoff explicit. These are latest official-Q&A facts recorded by the project team; the exact requirements register is maintained separately. Q86 includes community grants and excludes government grants; it does not establish incoming/outgoing funding direction. Map the actual buyer process before presenting Wimblo's inbound funder/request/award/receipt journey as sufficient.

Q38 identifies difficult communications and custom report creation while praising existing report robustness. Compare comfort, manual imports/exports, accurate accessible data and seamless communication outcomes, rather than inventing a deficient incumbent reporting engine. Q54 requires all stored data in reporting, July 1–June 30, authorized transaction-year reassignment and real-time/scheduled internal or emailed reporting; school-scoped report restrictions are not required for foundation staff. Q49 explicitly supports outside-CRM School Windows/Successfund CSV/Excel reconciliation and requires annual employee/configurable fundraising receipt approaches. Test those exact routes.

Q56 protects names/contact and calls for ten-year history; equivalent controls are acceptable and a pentest is not a stated requirement. Preserve Q56's North America/Europe hosting statement alongside later Addendum 2's US/Canada collected-data restriction and separate personnel conditions; apply the controlling source set rather than silently choosing the broader geography. Q17 makes AI optional with human review/disclosure. All these points are latest project-team official-Q&A observations, not vendor comparisons.

Q60 requires qualifying product experience/references in nonprofit school-district/higher-education environments. An established platform provider can supply those for a qualifying Dynamics/Salesforce implementation prime, with prime implementation references additional. Q85 still requires the prescribed provider/partner letter and authorization. Kode Kinetics' general implementation experience cannot replace new Wimblo product history.

Wimblo currently demonstrates connected local synthetic workflows and control tests. Prior baseline evidence reports 93 checks; newer work has separate bounded tests. A prior test count is not proof of production service, buyer migration, required custom report builder, live Mailchimp, MFA/SSO, approved hosting/privacy or qualifying product history. Every proposed advantage below requires proof and remains open until measured.

## Blackbaud Raiser’s Edge NXT

**Published strength.** Blackbaud describes unified supporter records, segmentation, gift processing, pledge/tribute/campaign support, moves management and embedded fundraising AI. Its official page also describes built-in engagement tools. [Raiser’s Edge NXT product page](https://www.blackbaud.com/products/blackbaud-raisers-edge-nxt).

**RFP-specific evaluation test.** Record a manual/imported donation with school/classroom splits and a linked pledge; reconcile received totals after void. Produce subsidiary/household and board reports from the same records. Show how the proposed configuration handles the district's disconnected processor, required user mix and incumbent conversion without requiring a new Stripe connection.

**Our current gap.** Wimblo lacks comparable production fundraising-service evidence, live communications, buyer-approved migration and verified custom-report creation, receipt configuration/annual employee receipts and reporting/identity breadth. Its product history and qualifying references are unestablished.

**Proof-required advantage.** A simpler buyer-specific operating path with transparent money/commitment/noncash semantics could be advantageous only if authorized users complete the same tasks with fewer errors, accepted reports and an accepted conversion. Current code demonstrates examples, not superior outcomes.

**Strategy.** Respect the platform's breadth. Compete on scoped adoption, actual report reconciliation and a fully costed implementation that matches district constraints. Keep AI supplementary; Blackbaud already publishes it. Do not claim Blackbaud is expensive, difficult or noncompliant without proposal-specific evidence.

## Bloomerang

**Published strength.** Bloomerang publishes supporter profiles/timelines, interactive dashboards, dynamic donor groups, giving insights, duplicate prevention, nightly address updates and AI-powered campaigns. [Bloomerang CRM](https://bloomerang.com/crm).

**RFP-specific evaluation test.** Follow a constituent who donates, volunteers and attends an event; preserve their household/subsidiary recognition without increasing received income. Inspect opt-out handling, historical donor-board giving and how configured communications/report exports reconcile to gifts. Verify the actual edition/module scope in the proposed offer.

**Our current gap.** Wimblo has local relationship reports and staff-managed activities, but lacks full household/deduplication authority, address-quality services, operational outreach and documented adoption outcomes.

**Proof-required advantage.** Wimblo's fund-centric traceability could suit school/classroom designations if evaluator-approved allocation/rollup cases, realistic designation volume and the required six-user operating model pass. That suitability must be tested against the buyer's data and roles.

**Strategy.** Do not attack donor engagement or ease of use. Position a working school-fund operating model with transparent reports and accepted migration; prove usability in equal tasks rather than using styling as a proxy. Tie any volunteer phasing to the buyer's permitted schedule and include all eventual scope in cost.

## DonorPerfect

**Published strength.** DonorPerfect publishes donor profiles, customizable dashboards, custom/standard and scheduled reporting, volunteer management, security options and Constant Contact integration. Its features page describes donor journeys and AI-assisted email content. [DonorPerfect features](https://www.donorperfect.com/fundraising-software/features/).

**RFP-specific evaluation test.** Build the buyer's required custom donor/designation report live, export the required formats and reconcile splits/voids. Show the actual Mailchimp workflow requested by the buyer, including mapping, contact preferences and synchronization behavior; a published alternative email integration does not answer that specific test.

**Our current gap.** Wimblo has defined reports and saved filters, not a proven arbitrary custom report builder spanning all stored data, authorized year reassignment or scheduled delivery. It does not currently demonstrate live Mailchimp synchronization or approved native reporting formats.

**Proof-required advantage.** Exact school/classroom allocation explanations and a concise staff journey could be useful after approved custom reporting, migration and communications are demonstrated. There is no established advantage while these requirements remain missing.

**Strategy.** Treat reporting as a strong competing capability. Win through a configured and accepted report pack plus conversion reconciliation, rather than asserting our saved views are equivalent to its published builder. Compare the offered Mailchimp route fairly; do not claim the product lacks Mailchimp from this page alone.

## Neon CRM

**Published strength.** Neon publishes donor profiles, reporting, automation, grant management, email/text tools and separate event/volunteer modules. Its official overview lists an API and native QuickBooks, Mailchimp and Eventbrite integrations, plus onboarding/migration options. [Neon CRM overview](https://neonone.com/solutions/neon-crm-overview/).

**RFP-specific evaluation test.** Demonstrate actual Mailchimp preference synchronization; show community-grant request/award/receipt distinctions, event attendance and volunteer timesheets. Verify module inclusion and the disconnected-processor workflow. Reconcile a sample incumbent conversion and full multi-location/school-fund reporting under the proposed package.

**Our current gap.** Wimblo's local grant, staff registration/shift and time controls do not establish Neon-equivalent public self-service, provider communications, migration or commercial support.

**Proof-required advantage.** A carefully configured school/classroom operating model with fewer reconciliation exceptions could win only with comparable source conversion and timed/evaluated tasks. The advantage is proposed evidence, not a claim that Neon is disconnected.

**Strategy.** Do not use “we connect modules” as a unique claim; Neon publishes that capability. Demand exact included-module, integration and operating-cost scope in an equal comparison. Prove institution-specific behavior while accounting for our missing service/integration evidence. Avoid claims that integrated processing is required by Neon; test the offered offline route.

## Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud

**Published strength.** Salesforce's fundraising documentation describes awarded grants/recurring donations/gift commitments, donation transactions, donor profiles, gift entry, campaign results and donor rollups, with edition/permission/setup guidance and implementation resources. [Get started with Fundraising](https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sfdo.fundraising_get_started.htm&language=en_US&type=5).

**RFP-specific evaluation test.** Require the offered configuration, not a generic platform slide: school/classroom splits, households/subsidiaries, community grants, required data-entry permissions, accepted custom reports and actual incumbent conversion. Identify which vendor owns configuration, integrations, privacy operations, support and the November 1 launch.

**Our current gap.** Wimblo lacks verified enterprise configuration/service history, broad authorization/reporting and qualifying platform references. A small local control plane does not establish comparable enterprise administration.

**Proof-required advantage.** A narrowly scoped workflow requiring less ongoing administrative effort could matter if configured task completion, change effort, support responsibility and total ownership cost are measured. Custom code alone does not establish simplicity or lower cost.

**Strategy.** Evaluate the implementation and licensed components rather than portraying Salesforce as a blank CRM. For a Q60 partner route, disclose the actual manufacturer and obtain its qualifying product experience/references; add the implementation prime's own references and satisfy Q85 letters/authorization. Do not use an implementer's background to imply Wimblo's product history.

## HubSpot partner approach

**Published strength.** HubSpot's nonprofit page publishes configurable CRM/pipelines, campaigns, content and automation, Marketplace fundraising/event connections, Breeze AI and nonprofit training resources. It explicitly describes custom-object/pipeline/integration approaches for nonprofit tracking and volunteer/event coordination. [HubSpot for Nonprofits](https://www.hubspot.com/nonprofits).

**RFP-specific evaluation test.** Have the proposed partner demonstrate pledge/grant receipts, split gift designations, subsidiary/household reporting and permission boundaries as persisted configured records. Identify every tier, integration and support owner. Show an approved route without Stripe-connected recurring billing: HubSpot's published Commerce route is not applicable merely because it exists.

**Our current gap.** Wimblo lacks HubSpot-equivalent operational marketing, ecosystem integrations, onboarding/training resources and production service evidence. Its present nonprofit objects are insufficient to declare superior configurability or time to deploy.

**Proof-required advantage.** Nonprofit money semantics could be clearer in Wimblo only if the same complex cases expose a measurable difference after the partner configuration is complete. Verify required custom reports and conversion first.

**Strategy.** Q20 is an anonymous participation signal; name no partner. Test configuration completeness and accountable ownership, not the “general CRM” stereotype. AI is shared market territory. No edition-independent price or nonprofit discount eligibility is assumed.

## NonProfitEasy / incumbent continuity

**Published strength.** NonProfitEasy's official Mailchimp page describes two-way integration, segment use, automatic contact updates and email history on the constituent record; its navigation identifies donor, event, pledge/grant and reporting areas. [NonProfitEasy Mailchimp integration](https://nonprofiteasy.com/integrations/mailchimp/).

**RFP-specific evaluation test.** Under authorized source access, inventory and reconcile actual exported constituent IDs/relationships, gifts/splits/voids, pledges, grant records, communication preferences/history, attachments and current/historical contracts. Test what is exportable before the November 30 cutoff. Determine the interim continuity plan for November 1, including missing exports and report dependencies.

**Our current gap.** Wimblo has generic gift import and local schema upgrades, not actual NonProfitEasy extraction, approved mappings, attachment/contract conversion or live Mailchimp parity.

**Proof-required advantage.** Better traceability or easier operations require a trial conversion with accepted control totals, relationship integrity, preserved preferences and usable historical reports. A clean synthetic demo cannot establish incumbent replacement value.

**Strategy.** Make migration trust and comfortable communications/report creation the centerpiece. The buyer describes incumbent reports as robust; compete on workflow improvement, not an invented reporting weakness. Respect switching costs and operational continuity; do not invent dissatisfaction or claim the incumbent lacks integration. Continuity is a strategic alternative, not a confirmed competing proposal. Do not contact source-vendor or foundation staff without explicit authorization and applicable procurement permission.
