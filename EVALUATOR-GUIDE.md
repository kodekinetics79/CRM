# Kinflect evaluator guide

Use this guide to review connected workflows in the local Kinflect pilot, tailored to Jordan Education Foundation. This is Kode Kinetics' synthetic proof of concept, not an official district product. Run the application using [README.md](./README.md), then sign in as `alex@foundation.example` with password `FoundationDemo!2026`. Starting data is synthetic; create only fictional records.

The steps below exercise persistence and validation as well as visible screens. Record any missing UI path or unexpected result as a pilot gap; do not infer functionality merely from a field label. Amounts shown in the application are currency values; the API stores integer cents.

## Ten review steps

1. **Create and revisit a constituent.** Add a fictional individual named “Evaluator Donor” with an example-domain email, household label, phone, segment, notes, and Email preference. Add an extra contact if offered. Save, reload the application, and reopen the record. Confirm the values persist and that related activity starts empty. Change the preference to Do not contact, then restore it to Email after the communications check in step 9.

2. **Review school and classroom designations.** Inspect an existing school designation and its classroom/program children. Add a fictional child designation under a school, with an account code. Confirm the parent-child relationship persists. Attempt a self-parent or cyclic relationship if the editor exposes the operation; the server must reject it. A designation used by a gift must not be deletable.

3. **Post a split gift and test its total.** For Evaluator Donor, record a $100 Cash gift with two allocations: $60 to one designation and $40 to another. Associate a campaign and add a fictional external reference. Save and confirm the constituent's giving history and gift list show the gift. Try a second $100 gift with allocations totaling $90; the application must reject the inconsistent total without saving it.

4. **Import gifts and test duplicate handling.** Open Import gifts, choose Download sample CSV, and retain the eight exact headers: `donorEmail,amount,type,method,date,designationCode,campaignName,externalRef`. Use a unique existing donor email and designation account code; amounts are dollars, dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`, and optional campaign names must match existing records exactly. Add a fictional external reference, choose Validate and preview, then confirm Import. Confirm imported gifts persist and affect relevant totals. Reimport the same nonempty external reference; it must be rejected rather than create a duplicate. Submit a batch containing one invalid row; no row in that batch should be committed. Import supports up to 500 rows and one designation per CSV row; split gifts use the individual gift editor. Actual NonProfitEasy mapping is not included.

5. **Export and void a financial record.** Download the available gift/report CSV export and open it in a spreadsheet. Check that the split gift, amount, type, and designations are represented as the export supports. Void the $100 gift with an evaluator reason, then reload. Confirm it remains traceable as Voided, is excluded from active totals, and is not physically removed. Use any local receipt/acknowledgment preview as draft output only; no email or payment is sent.

6. **Track a grant deadline.** Add a fictional foundation constituent if needed, then create a grant linked to that funder with an application deadline, reporting deadline, amount, stage, and notes. Move its stage from Preparing to Submitted. Reload and verify the dates and stage remain stored. Check the grant list/dashboard for the deadline. This workflow does not submit an application or send reminders.

7. **Capture volunteer time.** Create a volunteer record linked to a constituent with skills, shift, and an optional event. Open the volunteer, choose Clock in, and confirm the button changes to Clock out and the active clock persists after reload. Clock out and confirm hours update. A very short test shift may display zero after rounding; compare clock status and stored duration rather than expecting a whole hour. Repeated clock-in/out operations are prevented by the API, which can be checked separately in a technical review.

8. **Register and check in an event participant.** Create an event with capacity 1, future date, location, and a nominal ticket price. Open the event, select Evaluator Donor under Guest registration, enter a Table / seat label, and choose Register guest. Choose Check in and reload to verify registration/check-in persists. Confirm an already registered constituent is omitted from the picker and registration is disabled at capacity. Cancel the registration and confirm the slot is available. The API independently rejects duplicate/over-capacity requests. Ticket-price recording does not collect payment.

9. **Persist an event-linked task and preview a communication.** Create a follow-up task with owner, due date, priority, Evaluator Donor link, and the event from step 8. Open the task, choose Mark completed, and reload to verify its links/status. Create a communication draft or interaction log with a subject, date, channel, and body; open it to inspect the local name/email/date preview. While the constituent has Do not contact preference from step 1, verify a new draft is blocked. Restore Email preference and confirm a draft can be saved. Saving a draft/log does not deliver a message.

10. **Compare reports and role permissions.** In Reports, select Contributions by donor, Gifts by designation, or Gift ledger and filter by date, revenue type, and school year. Compare Cash, In-kind, Fee payment, and Employee giving; toggle Exclude fee payments. Confirm Voided gifts do not contribute to active totals and monetary versus in-kind noncash value is shown separately. Check First-time donors and Donor follow-up; follow-up identifies either stale giving or stale logged contact and excludes Do not contact. Volunteer hours and Grant pipeline use all current records, with gift filters disabled. Review the configured fiscal start month; July is a demonstration default. Choose Export CSV or Print / save PDF and verify the report scope. Sign out and sign in as `board@foundation.example` using the same demo password; review data and attempt a write through any offered editor—the server must deny it. Sign in as staff to confirm routine record operations work while administrative user/settings/backup operations remain restricted.

## Phase-two review steps

11. **Reconcile a pledge schedule.** Create a $1,200 pledge for Evaluator Donor with twelve monthly installments and a start date at or before today. Open its schedule: commitment, received, balance and overdue are distinct. Record a $100 monetary gift for the same donor, choose the Linked pledge and save. Confirm the oldest installment receives $100 and the outstanding balance becomes $1,100. A receipt that would overfulfill the commitment, a different donor, a fee or in-kind gift must be rejected. Void the linked gift with a reason: its history remains and the pledge balance returns to $1,200. Pledge detail uses today; Pledge balances in Reports permits an as-of date. Quarterly/annual dates, month-end clamping and remainder cents are calculated schedules, not automatic charges.

12. **Review recognition without duplicating income.** Create a parent organization, child organization or individual linked to it, and a household label shared by two fictional constituents. Record a monetary gift with a soft-credit recipient, then an in-kind gift using the In-kind method. Inspect Soft-credit recognition, Organization rollup and Household rollup. Direct monetary, soft monetary, direct noncash and soft noncash appear separately. Organization groups include full descendants. If direct donor and soft-credit recipient are in the same group, that gift counts once with direct precedence. The same gift may appear in different recognition groups: do not add group totals together as received income or issue additional tax receipts.

13. **Save and revisit report filters.** Choose a report/date/type/year scope, name it under Save current filters as, and choose Save new view. Reload and apply it through Apply a saved view. Confirm the filters and results reflect current records rather than an old snapshot. A board viewer can apply the shared view but cannot save or delete it. Staff/admin can delete a selected saved view. Views do not schedule messages or report delivery.

14. **Record a completed manual acknowledgment.** Use a posted non-fee gift whose donor permits contact. In Stewardship's Pending tab, choose Record acknowledgment. Enter the date, channel and notes describing a fictional action already completed by staff, then choose Record completed action. Confirm it moves to Completed, the gift shows acknowledgment metadata and Open recorded interaction reaches a linked Logged communication in donor history. Repeat acknowledgment must be blocked. Voided gifts, fee payments and Do not contact donors cannot receive a new action. This records activity only: it sends no email, confirms no delivery and issues no tax receipt.

## Volunteer ledger detail

Clock-out creates a completed dated interval, not just an accumulated total. Initial/imported hours remain a separate Historical entry with no invented date. In volunteer detail, choose Correct on a ledger row, enter corrected hours and a reason, save and reload. Confirm the original hours/reason remain visible and the total reconciles while timestamps stay unchanged. The API prevents simultaneous clocks for the same constituent. Reports → Volunteer time ledger filters completed intervals by clock-in date and school year while retaining undated historical hours separately. Historical rows in its CSV are explicitly labeled undated. The older Volunteer hours report still lists all current volunteer totals.

## Optional administrator checks

Review audit entries for the constituent, gift, void, and task changes. Where available, download the administrator snapshot and check that it contains business records without sessions, password hashes, or credentials. This export is a local snapshot, not a managed encrypted backup or evidence of a tested restoration/disaster-recovery process.

The API uses optimistic record versions. Two edits based on the same old version should produce a conflict for the later save; reload before retrying rather than overwrite another evaluator's change. Refer to [API-CONTRACT.md](./API-CONTRACT.md) for endpoints and fields when conducting a technical review.

## What this evaluation does not prove

These steps do not verify live Mailchimp/Google/Microsoft/Stripe connections, online payments, SSO/MFA, production hosting/encryption/backups/recovery, certification, institutional security acceptance, Learn Platform approval, a signed DPA, iBoss compatibility, or real NonProfitEasy migration. They also do not establish the RFP's experience/reference eligibility. Use [RFP-COVERAGE.md](./RFP-COVERAGE.md) to carry those gaps into the next implementation and procurement review.
