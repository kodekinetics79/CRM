## Current Utah cross-check

The official [Chapter 9 compilation, section 53E-9-309, pp.15–17](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title53E/Chapter9/C53E-9_2018012420180124.pdf) includes the 2026 amendment. It requires privacy-termination terms without fees, district notice of discovered unauthorized use within 30 days and termination no sooner than 30 days after notice if remedy/prevention are absent. At contract completion without renewal, return/delete personally identifiable student data is not dependent on a new district request; the stated parental written-consent exception needs legal review. Do not apply DPA retention exceptions as blanket permission to retain data contrary to controlling law. [Enrolled HB55](https://le.utah.gov/Session/2026/bills/enrolled/HB0055.pdf) specifies July 1, 2026 effectiveness. Legal/privacy owners must reconcile final contract, data scope and applicable exceptions before launch.

The published [R277-487 rule, sections 3/4/7](https://schools.utah.gov/adminrules/administrativerules/_administrative_rules_/_effective_rules/R277487EffectiveJuly2025.pdf) requires a documented contractor information-security program and appropriate safeguards, describes audit evidence routes, and ties student retention to approved schedules. Its LEA-to-Superintendent significant-breach report uses **ten business days from initial discovery**; that is a different party/trigger from Provider's DPA 72-hour confirmed-breach notice. Confirm current rule versions with district counsel; these duties do not collapse into one generic notification deadline.
