# Wimblo security, privacy and buyer acceptance register

September 13, 2026 · Wimblo 0.4.2 working source · Kode Kinetics LLC.

This is a product control/evidence register, not a certification, signed contract, submitted technical proposal or district approval. **Institutional production admission remains blocked.** Implemented means tested locally unless explicitly identified as a live configuration inspection. Preparing a policy does not implement or operate its controls.

## Sources and precedence

Reviewed the supplied 31-page `5-27TH05P5 RFP Nonprofit CRM Software FINAL 8-14-26.pdf`, 29-page `8-Attachment E Student Data Privacy Agreement- Caleb Olson 2026 NEW.pdf` (SDPC version 2.2), and `12-27TH05P5 - Addendum 2 09.10.2026.pdf`. The DPA's page 11 priority clause places Exhibit H above Exhibit G above standard clauses for Student Data conflicts. **No separate Exhibit H was found in the supplied 29-page attachment**; confirm the final executed agreement and subsequent addenda. Treat Addendum 2's US/Canada storage restriction as the conservative deployment boundary pending confirmation of the final agreements.

Student Data obligations apply to the education records/data actually processed. Donor-only data does not automatically become an education record; employee data has its own Utah disclosure obligations. Agree the data schedule before any migration. The [district buying guide](https://purchasing.jordandistrict.org/vendors/buying-guides/) directs unlisted software to privacy review and identifies its approved LearnPlatform library. Approval is not established by this document. [Federal privacy guidance](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/privacy-and-data-sharing) distinguishes consent and permitted disclosure conditions; the district must determine the applicable basis.

## Current Utah cross-check

The official [Chapter 9 compilation, section 53E-9-309, pp.15–17](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title53E/Chapter9/C53E-9_2018012420180124.pdf) includes the 2026 amendment. It requires privacy-termination terms without fees, district notice of discovered unauthorized use within 30 days and termination no sooner than 30 days after notice if remedy/prevention are absent. At contract completion without renewal, return/delete personally identifiable student data is not dependent on a new district request; the stated parental written-consent exception needs legal review. Do not apply DPA retention exceptions as blanket permission to retain data contrary to controlling law. [Enrolled HB55](https://le.utah.gov/Session/2026/bills/enrolled/HB0055.pdf) specifies July 1, 2026 effectiveness. Legal/privacy owners must reconcile final contract, data scope and applicable exceptions before launch.

The published [R277-487 rule, sections 3/4/7](https://schools.utah.gov/adminrules/administrativerules/_administrative_rules_/_effective_rules/R277487EffectiveJuly2025.pdf) requires a documented contractor information-security program and appropriate safeguards, describes audit evidence routes, and ties student retention to approved schedules. Its LEA-to-Superintendent significant-breach report uses **ten business days from initial discovery**; that is a different party/trigger from Provider's DPA 72-hour confirmed-breach notice. Confirm current rule versions with district counsel; these duties do not collapse into one generic notification deadline.

## Evidence required for a satisfactory submission and launch

1. Truthful C6–C8 narratives in RFP order, executed DPA and completed data/subprocessor/employee/framework exhibits; qualifying company/provider evidence remains a separate procurement gate.
2. District-approved access matrix and tested least-privilege reads/mutations/exports; MFA/SSO choice and recovery tests; 4–6-user acceptance.
3. Working durable hosted API; save/reload/restart evidence, TLS/proxy/session/CSRF checks and verified stored-copy residency.
4. Encryption/key-management evidence and a timed database restore with account/history/financial reconciliation, approved RPO/RTO and retention/disposal procedure.
5. Assessment/certification-equivalence evidence accepted by buyer; incident tabletop, staffed contact roster and practiced notice process; LearnPlatform/iBoss approval.
